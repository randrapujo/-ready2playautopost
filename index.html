About this application

Ready2Play AutoPost is a single-user, self-service tool that publishes the owner's own original short-form gaming news videos to the owner's own TikTok account (@Ready2play) using TikTok's official Content Posting API.

The account owner's system renders short gaming news videos (headline over official game footage with voiceover). This application uploads each rendered video and publishes it to the single TikTok account that authorized the app. It does not support third-party user accounts, does not scrape or republish other creators' content, and does not collect or store personal data beyond the OAuth tokens TikTok's login flow requires to act on the owner's behalf.
