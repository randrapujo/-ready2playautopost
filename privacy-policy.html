Privacy Policy

Ready2Play AutoPost

Details

This Privacy Policy explains what data the Ready2Play AutoPost application

("the App") handles and how it is used. The App is a single-user, self-service

tool that publishes original gaming news videos to its owner's own TikTok

account.

1. What the App does

The App renders short-form gaming news videos produced by the account owner

and publishes them to the owner's TikTok account through TikTok's official

Content Posting API. It is not a public platform and does not serve other

users.

2. Data we process

- OAuth tokens: when the account owner connects their TikTok account, the App

stores the access token and refresh token issued by TikTok. These are used

only to publish videos to that owner's own account and to refresh the

connection. We do not sell, share, or use them for any other purpose.

- Video files and captions: rendered videos and their captions are processed

temporarily in order to upload them to TikTok. They are not stored on

third-party services beyond the time needed to complete the upload.

- Contact details: the developer account's email address is shared with

TikTok as required by the TikTok for Developers program.

The App does not collect names, phone numbers, payment data, location data,

or any other personal information from any person.

3. What we do NOT do

- We do not track users or visitors.

- We do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

- We do not collect data from children.

- We do not share personal data with third parties other than TikTok, as

strictly required to perform the publishing function.

4. Data retention

OAuth tokens are kept for as long as the connection is in use and are deleted

if the owner disconnects the account or requests deletion. Uploaded video

files are retained on TikTok's servers under TikTok's own policies; we do not

host a copy after the upload completes.

5. Security

Tokens are stored locally on the owner's own machine with restricted file

permissions and are transmitted to TikTok only over HTTPS.

6. Your rights

As the sole user, you control your own data: you can disconnect the TikTok

account at any time from the TikTok app (Settings → Apps → connected apps),

which revokes all tokens held by this App.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with

a new effective date.

8. Contact

For questions about this policy or data deletion, contact the developer

account owner via the developer email listed in the app's public information

in the TikTok for Developers portal.
